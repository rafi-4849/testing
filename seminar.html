About the Seminar

Exploring the Frontiers
of Science & Technology

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) fields are at the heart of modern innovation and national development. As the world pivots toward an era of artificial intelligence, quantum computing, nanotechnology, and advanced materials, it becomes imperative for academics, researchers, and students to engage deeply with these cutting-edge disciplines.

This One Day National Seminar on "Cutting Edge in STEM" is organized by the Department of Physics in collaboration with IQAC, Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Smarak Mahavidyalaya, Muzaffarpur. It brings together eminent speakers, researchers, and young minds to deliberate on recent advances, research challenges, and the future trajectory of STEM disciplines.

The seminar serves as a platform for knowledge exchange, networking, and inspiring the next generation of scientists and engineers in Bihar and beyond.

Seminar Objectives

🔬
Advance Research Dialogue Foster discussions on the latest discoveries across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics & CS.
🤝
Academic Collaboration Build inter-institutional research partnerships and mentorship opportunities.
🎓
Student Empowerment Inspire undergraduate and postgraduate students to pursue careers in STEM research.
🌐
National Development Focus Highlight how STEM innovation drives economic growth and sustainable development in India.
